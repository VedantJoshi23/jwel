What we need from you

The website currently makes ten promises to customers that the business has not yet committed to. For each one, we need a simple answer: keep it — and we make the system honour it — or drop it, and we remove the wording. We have built an automated check that refuses to launch while any of these are unanswered, so none of them can quietly slip through.

Cash on delivery
Recommend: remove. You have already ruled it out, so the wording should go.
Tarnish-resistant plating
Your call alone. This is a claim about the product itself — either you stand behind it or we take it off the site.
"Dispatched within 24 hours"
Tell us the dispatch commitment you can actually meet, or we soften the wording.
"Arrives in 3–6 business days"
Cannot be honest until the courier integration is live. Suggest removing the specific days for now.
Returns require unworn items and original packaging
Confirm the shop will genuinely refuse a worn piece — otherwise the policy reads stricter than reality.
Customisation on select styles
Either someone answers customisation enquiries through the Contact page, or we remove the offer.
Free shipping over ₹999
Cheap once decided. Give us one number and checkout will apply it automatically — adjustable later without a developer.
"Jewel Box" subscription
This is a product, not wording. Either it becomes a separate piece of work, or the section and footer link come off.
Live order tracking
Cheap once decided. The order status timeline already works — we suggest simply describing that instead.
Newsletter sign-up
Either you choose a mailing provider and we connect the form, or the form comes off the footer.
The fastest path. Six of the ten can be settled in a single conversation — they only need a yes, a no, or a number. That would leave four: the plating claim, the subscription, customisation staffing, and the newsletter provider.

What we do on launch day

These are jobs for the day we go live, in order. They are already written into our deployment checklist, so nothing here is at risk of being forgotten.

  1. Register the shop's domain and connect the email sender Until this is done, order confirmations and refund notices do not reach customers. This needs a domain you own — a Gmail address will not work as a sender. It is the single most important launch-day item.
  2. Switch on the full search engine Search works today either way, but typo tolerance, filters and suggestions only come alive once this is running alongside the site.
  3. Align the two revenue figures The admin dashboard subtracts refunds; the reporting tool does not, so the two would disagree. Until we unify them, quote revenue from the dashboard only.
  4. Enter live payment credentials and run the go-live sequence The final step before the shop can take real money.

Paused, waiting on something outside our control

  • Courier integration and shipment tracking blocked
    Waiting on the courier account, which is currently blocked on their side. This is what stands between us and an honest delivery estimate.
  • WhatsApp and SMS notifications blocked
    Needs a WhatsApp Business account. The number we have today is an ordinary chat contact, which cannot send automated messages.
  • Fraud scoring withdrawn
    This existed to protect cash-on-delivery orders. With cash on delivery dropped, there is nothing left to score.

Improvements planned after launch

None of these block going live. They are listed so you can tell us which matter to the business and in what order.

  • Email customers when a return moves forward
    Today the customer sees the update in their account, but nothing tells them to look. Likely resolves itself once the email sender is connected.
  • Filters on the search results page
    Filtering by metal, category, certification and price is already calculated behind the scenes — it just needs a place on the page. A design question more than a build one.
  • Sorting search results
    Deliberately left out rather than shipped as a control that did nothing. Worth adding once we know how customers actually search.
  • Gift wrap chosen per item
    The system supports it end to end; the bag has no control for it yet.
  • Coupon rules stated in the published terms
    There is one rule customers can hit without warning. Small change, but it needs your wording.
  • How long a shared bag stays valid
    A business decision first — how long should a shared link keep working? — then we clear out the old ones automatically.
  • Tuning the recommendation engine
    The settings are adjustable without a developer, but we need real order volume before we know what to set them to.

Worth scheduling before launch

  • A screen-reader session with someone who uses one not done
    Automated accessibility checks pass across every screen, but they only catch part of the picture. A person testing with a screen reader is the other part, and no one has done it yet.
  • Agreeing what "fast enough" means not set
    We can hold the site to a page-speed target automatically, but only once someone decides what the target is.
