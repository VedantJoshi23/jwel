DejaVu Sans / DejaVu Sans Bold — bundled here because pdfkit's built-in
standard fonts (Helvetica etc.) have no glyph for ₹ (U+20B9), which
FEAT-CATALOGUE-EXPORT's PDF prices need. DejaVu Sans does.
